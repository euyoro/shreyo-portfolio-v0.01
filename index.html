I was supposed to be a scientist.

Growing up, I was fascinated by almost everything: zoology, chemistry, physics, art. I think that instinct has stayed with me. I still can’t look at something without wondering how it works, why it works, and whether it could work better.

That curiosity eventually became a career in design. I found in design a way to solve problems with both logic and creativity. Over the years, I’ve moved through product, UX, visual design, publication, branding, and motion, not because I wanted to collect disciplines, but because I’m a learner at my core. I like entering unfamiliar territory, understanding its rules, and figuring out what I can do with them.

Approach in design

My approach is simple: break a problem down to its elements, understand the system, study what already exists, and then build from there. I design with logic and guts. The logic comes from research, methodology, and metrics; the gut comes from patterns I’ve picked up by paying attention to the work, people, and world around me. I care deeply about quality and about seeing an idea through to the last mile, not just making something that looks right, but making sure it works.

I believe the safest way to be ordinary is to never be absurd. If you dare to make the unexpected choice, you might make something that actually stands apart.

Today, I’m a Product Designer working primarily across fintech and digital products, bringing together the analytical curiosity that started everything with the visual craft I’ve built along the way.
