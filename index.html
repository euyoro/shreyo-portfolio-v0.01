Hello there,
I'm Shreyo.

I work on the parts of fintech that are hard to make feel good. Reporting someone's rent to a credit bureau. Selling identity protection to a person who doesn't think they need it. Regulation sets the rules, the stakes are somebody's actual money, and the room to be clever is small.

I stopped waiting for engineering bandwidth to test an idea. I build them instead — not as prototypes but deployed, on a URL, with a database behind them. I don't think this makes me an engineer. I think it makes me a designer who wastes less time.
