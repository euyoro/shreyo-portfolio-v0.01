About

Designer first. Builder second. Both seriously.

I am Shreyo. I design financial products at Bright Money, and when the tool my team needs does not exist yet, I build that too.

I work on the parts of fintech that are hard to make feel good. Reporting someone's rent to a credit bureau. Selling identity protection to a person who does not think they need it. Regulation sets the rules, the stakes are somebody's actual money, and the room to be clever is small.

I stopped waiting for engineering bandwidth to test an idea. I build them instead, not as prototypes but deployed, on a URL, with a database behind them. Competitor Hub reached a production stack three days after I opened the repo and now runs the team's research cycle.

I do not think this makes me an engineer. I think it makes me a designer who wastes less time.

I would rather stand in the gap between designing a thing and shipping a thing than argue across it.
